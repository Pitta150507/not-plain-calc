# Not Plain Calc Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-08

Not Plain Calc is a local, offline calculator app.

## Data Collection

Not Plain Calc does not collect personal data.

The app does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit:

- Personal information
- Calculator input
- Calculation results
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Contact information
- Usage analytics
- Advertising identifiers

## Accounts And Login

Not Plain Calc does not use accounts, login, registration, or user profiles.

## Analytics, Ads, And Tracking

Not Plain Calc does not use analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, or third-party tracking services.

## Backend And Database

Not Plain Calc does not use a backend server or database. Calculations happen on the device.

## Network Access

Not Plain Calc is designed to work offline after installation. No calculator input or result leaves the device.

## App Store Privacy Statement

Data Not Collected.

## Independence Disclaimer

Not Plain Calc is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Apple Inc.
