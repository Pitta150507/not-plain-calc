FAQ

Does Not Plain Calc collect data?

No. It does not collect personal data, use analytics, show ads, track users, or send calculations to a server.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Calculations happen on device and the app does not require a network connection for calculator use.

What operations are supported?

Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, equals, percent, decimal input, clear, and delete.

How do I report a bug?

Email the support address with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, exact steps to reproduce the problem, expected result, and actual result.
