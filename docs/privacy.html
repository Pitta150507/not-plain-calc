Privacy Policy

Data Not Collected

Not Plain Calc is a local, offline calculator. It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

No Login

The app does not use accounts, sign-in, profiles, or user registration.

No Tracking

The app does not use analytics, tracking SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party tracking services.

No Ads

The app does not show ads and does not collect data for advertising.

On-Device Calculations

Calculator input and results are processed on your device. Not Plain Calc has no backend, no database, and no server that receives your calculations.

  • No personal data collected.
  • No calculator input leaves the device.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No ads or advertising data collection.
  • No user accounts or login.
